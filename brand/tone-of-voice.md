# Mani OS — Tone of Voice Style Guide

## Principles
1. **Clear and Concise**: Say what matters in fewer words.
2. **Helpful and Actionable**: End reports and alerts with concrete action targets (e.g. *"Hydrate now"*).
3. **Encouraging, Not Preachy**: Celebrate progress without nagging the user.

## Terminology Checklist

* Avoid generic alerts like *"Hydration threshold not reached"*.
  * **Brand Standard:** *"You are 250ml away from today's hydration target."*
* Avoid mechanical messages like *"Database snapshot updated successfully"*.
  * **Brand Standard:** *"System Snapshot saved successfully."*
* Avoid empty stats like *"Correlation coefficient = 0.81"*.
  * **Brand Standard:** *"Mani AI noticed your focus sessions tend to be longer on days you sleep more than 7.5 hours. Consider moving bedtime earlier."*

## Loading Transitions
Rotate these operating system bootstrap messages:
1. `Initializing Intelligence...`
2. `Loading Modules...`
3. `Building Dashboard...`
4. `Analyzing Activity...`
5. `Preparing Mission Control...`
6. `Synchronizing Events...`
7. `Generating Insights...`
8. `Optimizing Performance...`
